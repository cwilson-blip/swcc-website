Programs · Recording Studio

The Upper Room Studios

A hybrid recording facility at Southwestern Christian College, built around an SSL Origin 32 console. Every session includes a professional engineer.

Hybrid Recording Facility

Analog warmth, digital reach.

The Upper Room pairs a 32-channel SSL analog console and a rack of classic outboard gear with Pro Tools, Luna, and Logic — so a session can move freely between hardware and the box.

Production packages are available for songwriters, producers, vocalists, and musicians.
